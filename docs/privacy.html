Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-08-26

AppTiktok is a personal developer sandbox application, used only by the developer who registered it, to test integration with TikTok's developer APIs in TikTok Sandbox mode.

Any data obtained through TikTok's APIs (such as profile information or access tokens) during testing is used only for that testing, stored locally by the developer, and is not shared with any third party, sold, or used for advertising. Access and refresh tokens are stored outside of version control and are not published anywhere.
